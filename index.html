TF2 Patch Notes Generator

Fake but believable Team Fortress 2 update notes, continued by GPT-2 running entirely in your browser.

On phones this defaults to DistilGPT-2 to keep the first download smaller. Expect tens to a few hundred MB the first time (then cached). Older phones may be slow — if something runs out of memory, stick with DistilGPT-2.
